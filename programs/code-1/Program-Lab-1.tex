\Practicum{Inhuman Action in the Internet Age}{%
The \Parens{local} Olympic committee has made an online speed shooting simulation in order for contenders to practice for the upcoming Olympic sporting event.
Additionally, the person achieving the highest score in the simulator will qualify for the \Parens{local} Olympics online speed shooting competition, and due to an esoteric rule loophole, who ever is the qualifying person this year will win the gold medal by default.\\[5mm]%
{\hspace*{2cm}\href{recursion.ninja/target-practice}{https://recursion.ninja/target-practice}}\\[5mm]%
Reed Coil is a very boastful gamer and claims that no one can beat them at online speed shooting.
They assert that the best anyone can ever achieve on the simulation is a score of $500$, which coincidentally happens to be Reed's top score.\\[5mm]%
Most people agree that it would be unfortunate for someone as boastful as Reed Coil to win the gold medal in online speed shooting.
Such an outcome would reflect poorly on the community and dimminish its reputation.
Fortunately for the community, if any person can achieve a higher score than Reed's top score, they will unseat Reed from their Olympic qualification and take the qualifying seat for themselves.\\[5mm]%
\textit{Who amongst the CSCI 39531 class will out score Reed Coil; saving the comunity's reputation and taking the gold?}%
}
%
\Practicum{Instructions}{%
Complete the \texttt{aimbot-gold.py} script in order to automate playing the online speed shooting simulation.
In this programming assignment we will learn the basics of automation through simulation a Human Input Device (HID).
The tool we will be using to simulate a HID is the Selenium framework.
Selenium works by creating a new browser window controled my the ``automation driver.''
Within this browser window, there are many commands for locating, inspecting, and interacting with elents of the web browser's Document Object Model (DOM).
This automation task will require very basic usage of the mouse manipulation features provided by Selenium.\\[5mm]%
Complete the \textit{three tasks} within the provided \texttt{aimbot-gold.py} script.\\[5mm]%
Success is achieve once your completed \texttt{aimbot-gold.py} can \textit{reliably} achieve a score greater than $500$.%
}
